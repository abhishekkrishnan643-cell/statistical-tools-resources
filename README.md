Two-Way ANOVA Calculator
A free online Two Way ANOVA Calculator helps researchers analyze the effects of two independent variables on a dependent variable.

Use the calculator here:

https://freestatisticaltools.com/two-way-anova-calculator/

What is Two-Way ANOVA?
Two-Way ANOVA is a statistical method used to evaluate the impact of two independent variables and determine whether interaction effects exist between them.
